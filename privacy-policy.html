Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 11, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Nex Genix Creative Solutions ("NGCS", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our website. By using our services, you agree to the practices outlined below.

01 — Data We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it through the following channels on our website:

  • Contact Form — When you reach out to us, we collect your name, email address, and the details of your message or project inquiry.
  • Client Login Portal — Registered clients provide credentials (email and password) to access their dedicated dashboards. Depending on your project, this may also include business name, project notes, and communication history.

We do not collect any personal data passively through third-party advertising trackers or behavioral profiling tools.

02 — How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to project inquiries and client communications submitted via the Contact Form.
  • Managing and tracking ongoing client projects through the Client Login Portal.
  • Providing project updates, deliverable timelines, and revision records to the appropriate client.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Your data remains strictly within NGCS for operational use only.

03 — Data Security

We take the protection of your information seriously. The following measures are in place to safeguard your data:

  • All Client Dashboards are protected with unique, password-authenticated login credentials.
  • Access to client project data is restricted to the client themselves and authorized NGCS team members directly assigned to the project.
  • Our platform is hosted on GitHub Pages with HTTPS enforced, ensuring encrypted transmission of all data in transit.

While we implement industry-standard security practices, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage clients to use strong, unique passwords for their portal accounts.

04 — Cookies

Our website uses minimal functional cookies that are strictly necessary for the site to operate correctly. These cookies enable features such as session management on the Client Login Portal and do not track personal browsing behavior across other websites.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or analytics platforms that profile individual users. By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of these functional cookies.

05 — Questions & Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal data, please reach out to us directly:

  • Via our Contact Form on the website.
  • By emailing us at hello@nexgenixcreative.com
  • We are based in Zamboanga City, Philippines.

We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 3–5 business days.
