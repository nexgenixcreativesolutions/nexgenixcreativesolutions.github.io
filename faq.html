Pricing

4 questions

At Nex Genix, we don't believe in one-size-fits-all pricing. Every business has a different scope, timeline, and budget — so our rates are scoped per project after a free discovery consultation.

During that initial call (or via our intake form), we'll ask about your deliverables, target deadline, and complexity. From there you'll receive a fixed quote with a clear itemised breakdown — no surprise invoices, no hidden fees.

General starting ranges for reference:

  • Design Logo design from ₱1,500 / Branding packages from ₱5,000
  • Web Landing pages from ₱8,000 / Full websites from ₱18,000
  • Writing Business proposals from ₱1,200 / Content packages from ₱800/mo
  • Courses Free to ₱2,750 depending on the programme

All prices are quoted in PHP and can be settled via GCash or PayPal. USD pricing is available on request.

Revisions & Edits

4 questions

Standard revision inclusions by service type:

  • Logo Design — 3 rounds of revisions
  • Brand Identity Package — 2 rounds per deliverable
  • Web Design (mockup stage) — 2 rounds
  • Web Development (live build) — 1 round of consolidated feedback
  • UI/UX Design — 2 rounds of wireframe revisions + 1 round at high-fidelity
  • Video Ads — 2 rounds of edit revisions before final render

A "revision round" means one consolidated set of feedback — not one individual change. We encourage clients to batch their feedback clearly to make the most of each round.

Writing services include the following revision rounds:

  • Business Proposal — 2 revision rounds
  • Content Writing Package — 1 round per article/post
  • Website Copywriting — 2 rounds for the full copy deck

Revisions cover changes to tone, structure, accuracy, and length. Scope changes (e.g., adding entirely new sections not in the original brief) may be quoted as additional work.

Additional revision rounds beyond the included allowance are available at a flat rate:

  • Design revisions — ₱500 per additional round
  • Writing revisions — ₱300 per additional round

We'll always let you know before charging anything. In practice, most projects complete within the included rounds when the initial brief is clear — which is why we invest time in the discovery phase.
